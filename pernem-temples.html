Mulvir and Bhagavati Temples

The family's oldest remembered sacred geography is in Pernem, Goa. Hari Bhat and his ancestors are remembered as priests connected with Mulvir temple, and Dutta Joshi from the *1 branch continues to treat Mulvir Shiva temple and Bhagavati temple in Mulvir as the kuladevata practice.

These two temples help explain why the family story should not be reduced only to the later Dattatreya or Kolhapur Mahalakshmi belief. Mulvir and Bhagavati preserve the Pernem-side memory of worship, priestly duty and village deity tradition.
