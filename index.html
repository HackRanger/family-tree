Documentary history

The movement of a priestly family

Origin

Mulvir, Pernem

The remembered beginning of this lineage is priestly service at Mulvir temple in Pernem, Goa.

Pernem, also known as Pedne, sits at Goa's northern edge near Maharashtra, a region shaped by temple villages, border movement and old Konkani-Marathi cultural exchange.

Patronage

Kowad

A later turning point came when ancestors of Marathi writer Ranjit Desai are remembered to have brought Hari Bhat or his descendants to Kowad for regular pooja at the Desai residence.

Kowad is in Chandgad taluka of Kolhapur district, close to Belagavi.

Migration

Three Directions

After several generations in Kowad, the family story spreads into three broad geographies: Belagavi, Bangalore and northern Karnataka.

Bangalore relatives became associated with Karada Brahmin Sangha networks through Marathi language and community memory.

Bangalore

Babu Bhat on Surveyor Street

Babu Bhat, also remembered as Bapu Bhat, was a son of Purushottam Bhat. Family history identifies him as a government surveyor and resident of Surveyor Street in Bangalore.

A Bangalore Mirror local-history article gives the wider context of Surveyor Street as a place associated with surveyors who came to Bengaluru during city expansion.

Investigation

Narsoba and Dattatreya

Your Kowad investigation suggests a possible ritual confusion: a Narsoba temple in nearby Nittur may have been overlooked, while similarity of names led some ancestors to associate the lineage with Narsoba Wadi and Dattatreya worship.

Your branch

Sitaram > Balakrishna > Govind Rao > Umashankar > You

The scan stops before Govind Rao's generation, so this documentary restores the Mysore line from oral history. Govind Rao's sons are Seetaram, Umashankar, Nagaraja and Shashidhar. Seetaram's sons are Abhijith and Avinash, Nagaraja's son is Ritesh Joshi, and Shashidhar passed away in 2005 as a bachelor.

Mulvir Region

Pernem/Pedne is Goa's northern gateway, bordered by Maharashtra and connected by river, road and coastal movement. In family memory, this is the sacred starting landscape.

Kowad Region

Kowad sits in Chandgad taluka, Kolhapur district, near Belagavi. Its borderland position helps explain family movement toward Karnataka towns while keeping ties to Kowad temples and village memory.

Karada Brahmin Culture

Karhade/Karada Brahmins are associated with Marathi and Konkani-speaking regions of Maharashtra, Goa and Karnataka. For the Bangalore side, Marathi language appears to have helped preserve community identity after migration.

Family Deity

The remembered family deity is Kolhapur Mahalakshmi. This is kept distinct from the later Dattatreya/Narsoba thread, which may reflect a branch-specific devotional development.
