Joshi [Panashikar] Kovadkar Family

A Family Tree Documentary

This documentary gathers family memory, scanned genealogy, temple references and field investigation into one shared archive for the Kovadkar family.

Its purpose is to preserve what is known, mark what is uncertain, and make future corrections easier. Each topic now has its own page so the family tree, history, gotra, temple notes and source files can be edited independently.
