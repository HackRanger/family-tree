Kuladevata investigation

Nittur Narasimha Temple

Nittur Narasimha Temple, also referred to in family discussion as Yoga Narasimha or Narsoba temple, is important because it may explain one branch of the family's kuladevata confusion. Dutta Joshi, son of Vasant Joshi from the *1 branch, recalled that his mother believed the family deity could be connected with this temple in Nittur, Maharashtra.

This does not settle the question for the whole family. It records a living investigation: current belief includes Kolhapur Mahalakshmi and Dattatreya, Dutta Joshi continues to follow Mulvir Shiva temple and Bhagavati temple in Mulvir, and Nittur Narasimha remains a meaningful candidate remembered through his mother's account.

The renovation board in the images identifies the issuing group as Shri Narsinha Devalaya Jirnoddhar Mandal and Villagers, Nittur, meaning the Shri Narsinha Temple Renovation Committee and villagers of Nittur.
