Family oral-history note:
Dutta Joshi, son of Vasant Joshi from the *1 branch, recalled that his mother believed the family kuladevata could be connected with Yoga Narasimha temple at Nittur, Maharashtra. Dutta Joshi himself continued to believe Mulvir Shiva temple and Bhagavati temple in Mulvir as the kuladevata practice.
